How observing works

The pool is for small trades. Size belongs over the counter, where the price is agreed between two wallets and the chart never knows.

1

Post or find a bag

Sellers list a whole bag. Buyers bid on any wallet's bag, public or private.

2

Wallet approves

Every offer is signed by the wallet behind it and checked against its on-chain balance.

3

Swap in escrow

Tokens and SOL meet in an escrow program and settle together, or not at all.

4

Observe the chart

No market sell, no market buy. The chart stays exactly where it was.
